Problem
NOC teams saw customer complaints and network KPIs in separate tools, so service degradations were often spotted late, after a wave of complaints.
Approach
Connect both sources in one platform: validated ETL pipelines, anomaly detection, SHAP-explained disengagement prediction, behavioural segmentation and spatio-temporal analysis by governorate.
Outcome
A React NOC dashboard, a GIS complaint map, an admin console and an LLM assistant for engineers, with MLflow experiment tracking and CI/CD deployment.
